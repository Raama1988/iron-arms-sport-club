About Iron Arms Sport Club Thalangama

Iron Arms Sport Club Thalangama is a sports club based in Malabe, Sri Lanka.

The club was founded on 22 September 2020 with a focus on developing strength, fitness, teamwork and competitive sporting ability.

The club is particularly associated with Tug of War (කඹ ඇදීම) and aims to support athletes and teams through training, discipline, teamwork and sporting experience.

Founder & Leader

Ramesh Ranaweera

Ramesh Ranaweera is identified as the founder and leader of Iron Arms Sport Club Thalangama.

The club was developed from his interest, experience and determination in sports, strength training and physical fitness.

His sporting background includes experience in strength, fitness and competitive sports.

Ramesh Ranaweera – Sporting Achievements

  • Full Contact Karate National Champion – 2010
  • Powerlifting Champion – 2011–2019
  • Colombo District Bodybuilding / Physical Fitness Champion – 2013
  • District Swimming Champion – Backstroke, Freestyle and Butterfly

These achievements are included as part of the sporting background provided for the Iron Arms Sport Club website.
